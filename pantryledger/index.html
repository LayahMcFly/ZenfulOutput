Appearance

Your plan at a glance

Totals update as you add what you buy and how much of it you eat each day.

Your numbers

Set the calories you need and what you can spend on food each month.

Estimate my calories

A starting guess from a standard formula (Mifflin-St Jeor). People with fast metabolisms often need more, so check it against your weight trend below.

What you bought

Add each bag, box or pack. Search the food database, type a name like "oats" to use the short built-in list, or enter the calories from your package label.

Find a food

Searches Open Food Facts, a database of packaged products. Calories are per 100 g (per 100 ml for drinks) and are entered by volunteers, so compare them with your package label.

    Weight check-ins

    Log your weight now and then. The ledger compares your real trend to your goal and suggests a calorie change.

      Back up your ledger

      Everything is saved only in this browser on this device. Download a copy to keep it safe or move it to another device.
