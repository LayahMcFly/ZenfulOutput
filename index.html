License and privacy

The code is free software, released under the GNU General Public License, version 3. Every app keeps its data in your own browser. Here is when each one goes online:

  • Color Bloom: never goes online. Your progress stays in your browser.
  • Family App: posts, photos and videos are stored with our hosting provider, Appwrite, where only signed-in family members can see them.
  • Family Board: stays in your browser until you use shared mode, which stores tasks and sign-in emails with our hosting provider, Supabase, where only the people in your workspace can see them.
  • Family Recipe Book: only if you choose to save to your Google Drive, fetch new recipes from TheMealDB, or send ingredients to an AI service you set up.
  • Pantry Ledger: only when you search for a food, which sends the words you typed to Open Food Facts.
  • Party Pocket: never goes online. Scores and names stay in your browser.
  • Petal: never goes online. Entries stay in your browser.
