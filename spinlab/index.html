                    How to use SpinLab

                    SpinLab opens with one deck. Press Add another track under the waveform to open deck B and the crossfader so you can mix two songs. Press Load on a deck (or drop a song onto its waveform). Everything runs in this browser, so nothing is uploaded. Save your edits as a file with Save WAV or Save MP3, and record a live mix with Record live, which saves an MP3. Nothing goes online for any of them.

                    SpinLab: make your own songs

                    • The big SpinLab button on the home screen opens the studio. A song is made of tracks stacked on top of each other, like BandLab.
                    • + Instrument adds a track with any of SpinLab’s instruments. + Drums adds a drum kit. + Audio file adds a song or sound. + Voice adds a track for your microphone. You can also bring in a saved Piano creation or DJ project.
                    • Select a track, press Record and play the keyboard at the bottom (or your computer keys). The notes appear as blocks on the timeline.
                    • Open Notes to edit the blocks: click an empty spot to add one, drag to move it, drag its right edge to make it longer, and double-click (or press Delete) to remove it. Quantize lines your notes up with the snap grid.
                    • Each track has M (mute), S (solo), a volume slider and a ⋯ menu to rename, duplicate, pan, move or delete it.
                    • Click the numbers at the top of the timeline to move the playhead.
                    • Effects: pick a track and tap the Effects tab at the bottom, then Add effect. The effects browser works like BandLab’s: search, or pick a group (Recommended, One Knobs, Polish, Amps & Cabs, Compressor, Limiter, Rhythmic, EQ, Delay, Reverb, Modulation, Distortion, Lo-fi). Each effect you add becomes a card with its own knobs, an on/off switch, arrows to change the order, and an ✕ to remove it. Effects play live and are included when you export.
                    • Pitch: Pitch − / + in the top bar moves the whole song higher or lower, one semitone at a time (12 semitones is one octave). Each track also has its own Pitch knob in its Effects panel. Instrument tracks change their notes, so they stay perfectly clean. Audio, voice and drum tracks go through a pitch shifter, which keeps the speed the same.
                    • Seeing everything: The Piano, Notes and Effects tabs at the bottom open and close the bottom panel. Fit squeezes the whole song and every track onto the screen. − and + squeeze or stretch the timeline, and the arrows beside Tracks make tracks shorter or taller. On a phone, pinch sideways to squeeze or stretch the song, and pinch up and down to change track height. On a computer, hold Ctrl and scroll (add Shift for track height).
                    • Editing parts of a track: click a block of notes or an audio clip to select it, or drag across an empty part of a track with the mouse to select just that stretch. Then use Cut, Copy, Paste or Delete (or Ctrl/⌘ X, C, V and Delete). Paste goes in at the playhead on the selected track, and pressing it again adds another copy right after, which is handy for repeating a beat. Drag a selected part to move it along the track, or onto another track of the same kind. Ctrl/⌘ A selects everything on the track. Nothing here makes a new track.
                    • Save keeps the song in your SpinLab library. Export makes an MP3 or WAV file.
                    • Keys: Space play or stop, R record, Z X change octave, Delete remove the selected note or clip, Ctrl/⌘ Z undo, Ctrl/⌘ S save.

                    Home: DJ and Piano

                    • The home screen has three buttons. DJ is for mixing songs, Piano is for playing and making your own music (on a phone it turns sideways), and the third button opens Online Radio and Online Music. It shows the name of whichever side you used last.
                    • Each one has its own library under its button. Tap a saved item to open it, or use the pencil to rename and the bin to delete.
                    • Saving in DJ goes to the DJ library. Saving in Piano goes to the Piano library.

                    Online Music

                    • Online Music has two parts, switched at the top: Online Radio (live stations) and Music (official songs).
                    • Music searches Apple’s music catalog, so you can find artists like Taylor Swift. For now songs play as 30-second previews. ⋯ → Open in Apple Music plays a whole song there. Music has its own queue, playlists and a built-in Liked songs playlist (♡) that can’t be deleted. When a song ends, the next one in the queue starts.
                    • Tap the song’s picture in the player bar to open the full player. Scroll down for the lyrics, song info and what’s next in the queue. Swipe down or tap ⌄ to close it.
                    • On wide computer screens, SpinLab may show ads at the sides of Home and Online Music. They help cover the cost of full songs. DJ, Piano and phones never show ads. See the privacy page.
                    • Swipe the bottom player sideways or down to pause and hide it. Press play on any song to bring it back where you left off. Swiping the radio player away stops the station.
                    • Follow an artist from the full player, from ⋯ on any song, or from the artist card at the top of a search. Artists you follow are in the Artists tab and at the front of the name row, so one tap opens their songs.

                    Online Radio

                    • Live radio from thousands of free stations around the world, from the Radio Browser directory. Search by name, pick a genre or choose a country.
                    • It keeps playing when you lock your phone or switch apps, and the lock screen shows the station with play and pause.
                    • Tap ☆ to add a station to Favorites, the built-in playlist. Favorites can’t be deleted.
                    • Tap ⋯ on a station to Play next, Add to queue or Add to playlist. The Queue tab lets you reorder, remove, shuffle or clear what’s coming up, and ⏮ ⏭ on the player (and your lock screen) move through it. If a station in the queue goes off the air, the next one starts.
                    • The Playlists tab lets you make, rename, play, shuffle and delete playlists. SpinLab always asks before deleting a playlist or removing a station from one. Your playlists also show on the home screen.
                    • Online Music needs WiFi or mobile data. It’s the only part of SpinLab that goes online. Stations are live broadcasts, so some air their own ads.

                    Piano

                    • Tap the instrument button at the top to see the numbered list of instruments: keys, strings (violin, cello and more), guitars, woodwinds, brass, mallets and bells, choir, synths and a drum kit. The small arrows beside it step to the previous or next instrument ([ ] on a computer). Picking one plays a short sample.
                    • Every family has at least six instruments. The Drums family has six kits (acoustic, 808, dance, hand percussion, lo-fi and orchestral). With a kit, each key is a drum and is labeled with its name.
                    • The arrows on the right move the keyboard up or down an octave.
                    • Play with your fingers or mouse, and slide across the keys to glide. On a computer, A W S E D F T G Y H U J K O L P play notes, Z / X change octave, and holding Shift is the sustain pedal.
                    • Everything else is in Menu: Record, Play, the note view, Undo take, Clear, metronome, sustain, volume, reverb, Save, Library, Save WAV/MP3 and Send to DJ.
                    • While recording or playing back, a small pill at the top shows the time. Tap it to stop. R records, Space plays and stops.
                    • Record again to add a layer on top while earlier takes play. Undo take removes the last layer.

                    Projects and the library

                    • Type a name in the box at the top, then press Save (Ctrl/⌘ + S). If you leave it blank, the project is named Untitled 1, Untitled 2 and so on.
                    • A project keeps both decks’ songs with your edits, hot cues, loops, BPM, speed and every knob.
                    • Library lists your saved projects. Open, rename or delete them there, or start a new one.
                    • Projects live in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser’s site data deletes them.

                    Save vs. Record live

                    • Save WAV / Save MP3 save the song with your waveform edits (cuts, fades, reverse).
                    • Record live captures what you hear while you play: speed, EQ, effects, loops and both decks mixed together.

                    Videos

                    • Load a video (MP4, WebM, and on most computers MOV) the same way as a song. SpinLab pulls out the sound and puts it on the deck. Nothing downloads until you press Save WAV or Save MP3.

                    Waveform

                    • The ruler (the strip with the times, above the waveform) moves the white playhead. Click it or drag along it.
                    • The waveform is for selecting. Drag across it to select a part. Drag a selection’s edge to adjust it. Click once to clear the selection. When nothing is selected, a click moves the playhead.
                    • Snap makes selections land on the beat (¼ beat to 1 bar). Bright lines are bars, faint lines are beats. If the grid is off, put the playhead on a kick and press Beat 1 here.
                    • Type exact Start and End times in the selection bar. Play it plays just the selection. Repeat ×2 doubles it for stutters. Silence mutes it.
                    • Shift + ← → grows or shrinks the selection by one snap step. P plays the selection. Esc clears it.
                    • Scroll the mouse wheel (or use the zoom buttons) to zoom in around the cursor. Shift + wheel scrolls sideways.
                    • The thin strip under the waveform shows the whole song. Click it to jump anywhere.
                    • Copy something on deck A and paste it into deck B. Paste goes over the selection, or in at the playhead when nothing is selected.

                    Decks

                    • Speed changes tempo and pitch together, the way a turntable does. Pick ±8%, ±16% or ±50% for the slider range.
                    • Pitch (in Effects) moves the song higher or lower by semitones without changing its speed. Turn on Key lock to keep the song in the same key when you change its speed. Save WAV and Save MP3 include the new pitch.
                    • Sync matches this deck's tempo to the other deck. Tap lets you tap the beat if the detected BPM is off, or type the BPM in the box.
                    • Hot cues: tap an empty pad to save a spot, tap it again to jump there. Right-click or hold a pad to clear it.
                    • Cue: while stopped, it marks the cue spot. While playing, it jumps back to the cue spot and stops.
                    • Brake slows the record to a stop like a turntable losing power.

                    Keyboard

                    • The deck you touched last is the active one (it gets a colored border). Tab on its own moves between controls as usual.
                    • Space play or pause · C cue · 1–4 hot cues · [ ] switch deck (when deck B is open)
                    • Ctrl/⌘ + X C V cut, copy, paste · Del delete · Ctrl/⌘ + Z undo, + Shift redo · Ctrl/⌘ + A select all
                    • ← → nudge the song back or forward one second
                    • Knobs: drag up or down, use the arrow keys, or double-click to reset.
