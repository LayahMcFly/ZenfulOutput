Your library

Projects are saved in this browser on this device, with their songs, edits, cues, loops and knob settings. Nothing is uploaded.

    How to use SpinLab

    SpinLab opens with one deck. Press Add another track under the waveform to open deck B and the crossfader so you can mix two songs. Press Load on a deck (or drop a song onto its waveform). Everything runs in this browser, so nothing is uploaded. Save your edits as a file with Save WAV or Save MP3, and record a live mix with Record live, which saves an MP3. Nothing goes online for any of them.

    Projects and the library

    • Type a name in the box at the top, then press Save (Ctrl/⌘ + S). If you leave it blank, the project is named Untitled 1, Untitled 2 and so on.
    • A project keeps both decks’ songs with your edits, hot cues, loops, BPM, speed and every knob.
    • Library lists your saved projects. Open, rename or delete them there, or start a new one.
    • Projects live in this browser on this device. Clearing the browser’s site data deletes them.

    Save vs. Record live

    • Save WAV / Save MP3 save the song with your waveform edits (cuts, fades, reverse).
    • Record live captures what you hear while you play: speed, EQ, effects, loops and both decks mixed together.

    Videos

    • Load a video (MP4, WebM, and on most computers MOV) the same way as a song. SpinLab pulls out the sound and puts it on the deck. Nothing downloads until you press Save WAV or Save MP3.

    Waveform

    • The ruler (the strip with the times, above the waveform) moves the white playhead. Click it or drag along it.
    • The waveform is for selecting. Drag across it to select a part. Drag a selection’s edge to adjust it. Click once to clear the selection. When nothing is selected, a click moves the playhead.
    • Snap makes selections land on the beat (¼ beat to 1 bar). Bright lines are bars, faint lines are beats. If the grid is off, put the playhead on a kick and press Beat 1 here.
    • Type exact Start and End times in the selection bar. Play it plays just the selection. Repeat ×2 doubles it for stutters. Silence mutes it.
    • Shift + ← → grows or shrinks the selection by one snap step. P plays the selection. Esc clears it.
    • Scroll the mouse wheel (or use the zoom buttons) to zoom in around the cursor. Shift + wheel scrolls sideways.
    • The thin strip under the waveform shows the whole song. Click it to jump anywhere.
    • Copy something on deck A and paste it into deck B. Paste goes over the selection, or in at the playhead when nothing is selected.

    Decks

    • Speed changes tempo and pitch together, the way a turntable does. Pick ±8%, ±16% or ±50% for the slider range.
    • Sync matches this deck's tempo to the other deck. Tap lets you tap the beat if the detected BPM is off, or type the BPM in the box.
    • Hot cues: tap an empty pad to save a spot, tap it again to jump there. Right-click or hold a pad to clear it.
    • Cue: while stopped, it marks the cue spot. While playing, it jumps back to the cue spot and stops.
    • Brake slows the record to a stop like a turntable losing power.

    Keyboard

    • The deck you touched last is the active one (it gets a colored border). Tab on its own moves between controls as usual.
    • Space play or pause · C cue · 1–4 hot cues · [ ] switch deck (when deck B is open)
    • Ctrl/⌘ + X C V cut, copy, paste · Del delete · Ctrl/⌘ + Z undo, + Shift redo · Ctrl/⌘ + A select all
    • ← → nudge the song back or forward one second
    • Knobs: drag up or down, use the arrow keys, or double-click to reset.
