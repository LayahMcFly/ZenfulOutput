SpinLab Privacy Policy

Back to SpinLab

Last updated November 19, 2026

The short version

SpinLab has no accounts and no sign-up. Your songs, recordings, projects, playlists and settings stay on your own device and are never uploaded to us. The online parts (Online Radio, Music, lyrics and ads) connect to outside services, which are listed below.

What stays on your device

SpinLab saves your DJ projects, Piano creations, radio favorites, music playlists, liked songs, followed artists, queues and settings in your browser's own storage on your device. Songs and videos you import are processed on your device and are never sent anywhere. We can't see any of it. Clearing your browser's site data, or uninstalling the app, deletes it.

Online services SpinLab connects to

DJ and Piano work fully offline. These features connect to the internet only when you use them:

  • Online Radio looks up stations in the free Radio Browser directory and plays the station you choose straight from that station's own server.
  • Music sends your song and artist searches to Apple's iTunes Search service and plays Apple's 30-second previews. "Open in Apple Music" takes you to Apple's website or app. See Apple's privacy policy.
  • Lyrics are looked up by song title and artist in LRCLIB, a free community lyrics library.
  • Request a feature sends what you type in that form (the part of SpinLab you picked, your idea and, only if you add it, your email), plus the SpinLab version and the type of device and browser you use, to the SpinLab team by email through FormSubmit. Nothing is sent until you press Send request.
  • Support SpinLab buttons open Cash App. Any tip happens on Cash App under its own privacy policy.

Like any website, these services can see basic connection details such as your IP address when your device contacts them.

Advertising

On wide computer screens, SpinLab may show ads from Google AdSense at the sides of the Home and Online Music screens. Ads never appear in DJ, Piano or on phones.

  • Google and its partners use cookies to serve ads based on your visits to this and other websites.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies lets it and its partners serve ads to you based on your visits to SpinLab and other sites on the internet.
  • You can turn off personalized advertising in Google's Ads Settings, or visit aboutads.info to opt out of some other companies' personalized ads.
  • Learn more in How Google uses information from sites that use its services.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown.

Children

SpinLab does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. SpinLab itself has no accounts and stores nothing about you on a server.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change too. For questions, open an issue on SpinLab's GitHub page.
